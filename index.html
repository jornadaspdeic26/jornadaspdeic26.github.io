02 / THE EVENT

Research progress.
Exchange of ideas.
Stronger collaboration.

Jornadas PDEIC brings together supervisors, DEI faculty and the PDEIC coordination team for an open exchange on doctoral research across the department.

PhD students and their supervisors play a central role in DEI’s research. The Jornadas offers an opportunity to discover the topics they are working on, discuss the progress of their doctoral projects and recognize their achievements. By bringing these perspectives together, we aim to encourage collaboration across research groups and identify difficulties or support needs early.

The initiative combines research exchange with progress monitoring. It does not formally assess or grade students; those functions are served by the established doctoral assessment procedures at Técnico.

How will it work?

The day will comprise five sessions, one per DEI scientific area. Supervisors will briefly present each scheduled student’s research and progress during 2026, followed immediately by questions and discussion with the panel and audience. The panel will be chaired by the PDEIC coordinator and include representatives of all five scientific areas. All DEI faculty are welcome to attend and contribute.

Given the number of students, this edition will discuss projects of students who will have completed at least 18 months of doctoral studies by the event date—the recommended timeframe for completing the CAT.

Before the event, supervisors and their students will be asked to submit supporting material for the presentations and discussion. Coffee breaks and lunch will provide further opportunities to exchange ideas and explore connections across research groups.

04 / PREPARE FOR THE JORNADAS

One day. Two distinct roles.

Supervisors lead the discussion. Students help make the research and its progress clear.

FOR SUPERVISORS

You give the presentation.

  1. Check the program. Verify the students assigned to you, their scientific area and their time slots. Contact the PDEIC coordination team to confirm your availability or request corrections.
  2. Review the year with your student. Discuss research progress during 2026, the current situation and plans for 2027. You will be asked to provide a short summary of progress and any risks or support needs. In parallel, the student will submit research information that will be used to generate a single presentation slide.
  3. Keep the presentation to two minutes. Use the generated slide to introduce each student’s work and progress. Each presentation is followed immediately by 1½ minutes of questions and discussion.

If you cannot attend, contact the coordination team to arrange a suitable alternative, such as a co-supervisor.

FOR STUDENTS

Provide the information for your research update.

  1. Review your progress with your supervisor. Discuss what you achieved in 2026, any difficulties and your next steps for 2027.
  2. Submit your research information. You will be asked to provide information about your thesis topic, main results in 2026 and plans for 2027, together with relevant publications and research outputs. This information will be used to generate a single slide for your supervisor’s presentation.
  3. Coordinate with your supervisor. Ensure the submitted information accurately reflects your work and supports the update they will present.

You are not expected to give a presentation or attend the review sessions in this first edition. Any later public research profile will be handled separately, with authorization for dissemination.

answered.

Is this a student presentation event?

No. Supervisors present the work and discuss progress with the panel and other supervisors. Students contribute supporting material and do not attend the review sessions in this first edition.

Is this an assessment or a replacement for the CAT?

The Jornadas supports annual progress monitoring, dialogue and follow-up. It does not replace the CAT or the thesis defense. The committee’s written opinion identifies regular progress or a need for closer monitoring.

Why is my student not listed?

The preliminary schedule excludes students with less than 18 months of studies on 11 December 2026. Other cases may require confirmation by the coordination team. Absence from this day’s schedule does not mean the program will stop monitoring a student’s progress. Contact the organization if a listing appears incorrect.

How much time is available for each student?

Each slot lasts 3½ minutes: a two-minute supervisor presentation followed immediately by 1½ minutes of discussion. Sessions also include buffer time.

What if the supervisor cannot attend?

Contact the coordination team in advance to arrange an alternative presenter, such as a co-supervisor. Please do not assume a substitution has been recorded until it is confirmed.

When and where should materials be submitted?

Detailed instructions, templates, the submission channel and deadlines will be announced. At this stage, plan to submit student research information for a generated presentation slide and a short supervisor paragraph.

Will research information and committee opinions be public?

Detailed research profiles are planned for a later phase. Scientific information intended for dissemination will be handled with the appropriate authorization. Confidential opinions and reserved information will not be published.

Where will the event take place?

The event is planned at Instituto Superior Técnico. The room and arrival instructions will be announced here.

How do I request a correction to the program?

Please contact the PDEIC coordination team with the student’s name, current slot and requested correction. We will collect corrections until the deadline indicated in the announcement and then revise the program. If further corrections are needed after that deadline, please contact the organization as soon as possible.
